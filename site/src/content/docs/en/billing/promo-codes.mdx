The **Promo codes** section, with the "Codes" and "History" tabs.

## Kinds of codes

| Kind | What it gives |
|---|---|
| Bonus days | days added to the subscription term, right away |
| Bonus traffic | traffic added to the main limit or to a chosen pool, right away |
| Percentage discount | from 1 to 100% off a plan or traffic package at checkout |
| Fixed discount | an amount in rubles or Stars at checkout |

A subscriber enters the code in the Mini App; the bot opens it with the "Promo codes" button, already signed in. A single "Apply" button accepts both bonus and discount codes: a discount applies at checkout and is reserved for that time (30 minutes by default). All activations are visible on the "History" tab.

## Limits

- Start and end of validity.
- A total activation limit and a per-user limit.
- A minimum order amount and a maximum discount, in the code's currency.
- Only selected plans.
- First purchase only, new users only (for discounts).

## Discounts and payment methods

A discounted invoice can be paid only with a method that can refund: Telegram Stars or a marketplace adapter that declares refund support. If the payment arrives after the promo code reservation has expired, Arikan refunds it on its own. For other methods, the buyer is asked to choose a different payment method.
