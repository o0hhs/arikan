:::note[New in 0.5.2.0]
The front page templates are new in 0.5.2.0.
:::

Whoever opens the panel's address without the secret path sees the server's answer. A bare 404 with no page at all gives away that this is not a site: the panel answers so, and scanners know it. An ordinary page makes the server look like any ordinary site.

## Pick a template

**Settings → Security → Front page**: three tiles, the choice is saved at once.

| Template | What a stranger sees |
|---|---|
| Empty (404) | a 404 with no page, as before |
| Maintenance | a "Scheduled maintenance" page: the site is down for upkeep and back soon |
| Cat clicker | a small game: pet the cat and collect treats. Progress stays in the visitor's browser |

The **Open the front page** link under the tiles opens the panel's address without the secret path, so you see what a stranger sees. The admin panel's secret path does not depend on the template.

## Your own site wins over a template

If `/opt/mikan/data/panel/www` holds your files, the panel shows them, and the chosen template does not apply. The card then says "data/panel/www holds files of your own". How your own site works (paths, `404.html`) is in [Domain and certificates](/mikan/en/start/domain/#your-own-site-on-the-domain). This folder is not part of a backup, see [Backups](/mikan/en/operations/backups/).
