Most questions get solved in the [forum](https://t.me/+I2JFR7DbPow1ZmQy), and help there is free. Sometimes, though, a problem is only visible from the inside: someone has to open the panel and look. That is what paid support in the bot [@MikanSup_bot](https://t.me/MikanSup_bot) is for. You open temporary access to your panel and an operator deals with the problem. No password, SSH or server keys are needed.

This page is the full set of rules. Before you pay, the bot asks you to press "Accept the rules", and that button means you have read the text below and agree with it.

## How it goes

<Steps>

1. Tell the bot what happened. The AI answers first, from the knowledge base. Often that is enough and you pay nothing.

2. The answer did not help: press "Need an operator", describe the problem in one message and, if you can, send the output of `mikan doctor` or the "Check node" report.

3. Read the rules, press "Accept the rules" and choose a plan. The bot sends a payment button: a card, SBP or Wallet through Tribute, or crypto through @CryptoBot.

4. The bot learns about the payment by itself and opens the ticket. The operator sees it in their queue.

5. In the ticket press "🔑 Support access", open access in the panel and type the code from the bot. The panel hands the access to the ticket itself, the operator checks what is visible and gets to work.

6. When it is solved, the ticket is closed and the access link is erased.

</Steps>

## What is included

Help with mikan itself:

- installing and updating the panel and nodes;
- setting up protocols, cascades, WARP, certificates and the domain;
- subscriptions and links, the subscription page;
- the panel's Telegram bot and payments in the panel;
- errors in the panel and on nodes: logs, diagnostics, restart.

## What is not included

This does not mean we will not point you in the right direction. But looking into these is not a ticket, and the operator does not enter your panel for them:

- the hosting provider and its network: an unreachable server, a bad route, ports closed on the provider's side;
- blocking by ISPs and in particular networks;
- client apps: Happ, v2RayTun, Clash and others. We check what the panel serves, but we do not set up the apps themselves;
- other services and programs on the server besides mikan;
- code changes and new features, for those there are the [project goals](/mikan/en/support/);
- forks and unofficial builds.

If it turns out the cause is outside mikan, the operator will say so plainly. The ticket still counts as used: the time went into the diagnosis.

## No refunds

Money paid for support is not refunded: not for a one-off, not for a subscription, not for unused tickets. We cannot promise a result in advance, because the cause of a problem often becomes clear only after diagnosis. Paying means you agree to this.

The service counts as provided from the moment the operator starts working on the ticket. The ticket history, the operator's action log and the terminal recordings are kept as proof. If a payment is disputed through the bank (a chargeback), paid support is closed for that account for good.

You can check whether this kind of help fits you for free: the AI in the bot answers without payment, and the forum stays open to everyone.

## Access to the panel

The operator needs access to the panel, and you open and close it yourself: **Settings → Support**. How it works in detail: [Support access](/mikan/en/operations/support-access/).

- Choose the duration: 2, 6 or 24 hours. You can revoke access earlier with one button.
- Choose the level. **View** is read-only: the operator sees nodes, logs and diagnostics but changes nothing. **Change** also lets them edit settings, nodes and official addons, start updates and restarts. If the fix needs no changes, leave it on view.
- The **extended mode** is a separate switch that takes your password: only with it can the operator issue a node's key, change a node's address or domain, set an own certificate, change payments, payment details and balances, hand the bot to an addon or install an addon from outside mikan's catalog. It is rarely needed, and we will say so when a fix cannot do without it.
- The **Show client data** checkbox is off by default. Then the names and Telegram of subscribers are hidden, and subscription links and keys are masked. Turn it on only if the problem cannot be solved without it.
- The access opens by a one-time code from the ticket: the panel checks the code with the bot and hands the access to it itself. The link is never shown to you, there is nothing to forward, and nobody else can be let in this way.
- The support bot learns the secret address of your admin panel. When the support is over, you can press "Issue a new link" on the "Panel link" card.
- Only one access is active at a time. A new one cancels the previous one.

Access covers all nodes of your panel, so there is no need to set up each one separately.

Nobody gets into the panel without your button: the project has no standing access to your servers, and the panel reaches the support bot only when you open the access. If you do not need the feature at all, turn off the "Allow support access" switch on the same card or run `mikan support-access off` on the server: then no access can be opened even from the panel, and no support terminal is installed on the server.

### What the operator can never do

Even at the "Change" level in the extended mode these stay closed:

- the password and two-factor protection;
- API keys and signing in with them;
- creating or handing out new support access;
- resetting the panel's secret path;
- the backup to Telegram;
- the panel's audit log.

### Log and notifications

- Every operator action goes into the panel's audit log marked "support". The access card shows when they signed in and what they did.
- If the panel's Telegram bot is on, you get a message when the operator signs in and out.
- You can revoke access at any moment. The operator loses it at once and open sessions are closed.
- When the work is done, the operator presses "Finish and leave" and the access closes.
- Access ends by itself when the time runs out.
- While access is active, the panel shows a "Support is connected" banner.

## A server without SSH

Work on the server goes through the panel: logs of the panel and nodes, running `mikan doctor` and restarts. We never ask for the server password or an SSH key.

### Terminal

If the panel is not enough, you can turn on the **Terminal** level. It is full access to the server, so it works differently:

- it is off by default and only you can turn it on, confirming with your password and a two-factor code;
- you mark the servers where it is allowed: the panel server or chosen nodes;
- it works only for the length of the access and closes on revoke, on expiry and after 15 minutes of idling;
- the whole session is recorded, input and output with timestamps, and you can watch the recording in the panel. Recordings are kept for 30 days and then deleted;
- the terminal itself leaves nothing behind when it ends: its processes are stopped, it opens no ports, and SSH is not needed.

Keep in mind that root can change anything on the server, including adding a key, a cron job or a user and editing the recording itself. The recording lets you check what the operator did, but it is not a technical guarantee. Watch it after the session and open the terminal only for the time of the ticket.

The terminal is rarely needed. We will not ask you to turn it on if the task can be done without it.

### The panel is down

If the panel is down or a node is unreachable, access cannot be opened. Then the bot gives you a ticket code, and you run one command on the server:

```bash
mikan support <code>
```

It collects the output of `mikan doctor`, `mikan status` and the tail of the logs with secrets cut out, and sends it to your ticket. The code is single-use, tied to the ticket and lives for 24 hours.

## What data is kept

- **The ticket**: subject, description, diagnostics and the talk with the operator. The texts of a closed ticket are erased 30 days after it is closed. Unpaid ticket drafts are deleted after 30 days.
- **The access link** is stored encrypted and erased when the ticket is closed or the access ends. It never gets into logs.
- **Terminal recordings**, if you turned it on: 30 days, and in the panel only you can see them. They are kept on your server, so during a terminal session the operator has root access to them too.
- **Payments**: the order number, amount, plan and dates. They are needed to match a payment to a ticket and to count the subscription limit. We have no access to your card details: Tribute handles the payment.
- **Your subscribers**: the operator sees their names, Telegram and links only if you ticked "Show client data".

You can ask to delete the text of a ticket earlier in the ticket itself. Payment records stay: they are needed for bookkeeping.

- The operator acts only with your consent. If you ask them to stop, they stop.
